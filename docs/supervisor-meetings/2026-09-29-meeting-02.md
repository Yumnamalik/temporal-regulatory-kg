## Ideas Presented

### Idea 1: Temporal Regulatory Knowledge Graph using Ontology and LLM

A system for the problem of changing government rules and regulations. Laws, rules, notifications, amendments and policies are spread across many documents and change over time, so it is hard to know which rule currently applies and what changed from an earlier version.

- An **ontology** represents concepts such as laws, regulations, amendments, requirements, departments, services, dates and obligations.
- A **Knowledge Graph** represents the relationships between these concepts along with their validity periods.
- The system identifies an amendment, determines when it became effective, compares it with the previous rule, and traces which services or requirements are affected.
- An **LLM** lets users ask natural-language questions such as "What changed in this rule?", "Which rule was applicable in 2025?" and "What services are affected by this amendment?", and generates an evidence-grounded explanation based on the Knowledge Graph and official source documents.
- **Main objective:** a context-aware and explainable system for detecting regulatory changes, determining applicable rule versions, and identifying their potential impact over time.

### Idea 2: Evidence-Grounded PCOS Information Assistant

A system combining an LLM, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and a structured PCOS Knowledge Graph to provide reliable, evidence-based information about PCOS.

- Symptoms, risk factors, diagnostic criteria, complications, treatments and related conditions are represented in an ontology and Knowledge Graph, with trusted medical sources as the evidence base.
- For each question, the system identifies relevant concepts, retrieves supporting information from the Knowledge Graph and evidence sources, and gives this context to the LLM to generate the answer.
- Key focus: hallucination reduction and evidence verification. The system shows the evidence or graph relationships behind its answer and abstains when evidence is insufficient.
- **Main objective:** to investigate whether Knowledge Graph and RAG-based grounding improves the factuality, explainability and reliability of LLM-generated PCOS information compared with a standalone LLM.
- The system provides educational/decision-support information only and does not replace professional medical diagnosis.
