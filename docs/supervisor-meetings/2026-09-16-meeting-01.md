## Meeting Purpose

To discuss the project group structure and the initial organization of the Final Year Project.

## Group Merger

The students were initially working in two separate groups:

- **Group 1:** Yumna Aziz and Hamna Tariq
- **Group 2:** Noor Fatima and Aqsa Bibi

During the meeting, the supervisor proposed merging both groups into a single four-member Final Year Project team, to work on **LLM-Integrated News Forecasting System Using Ontology & Knowledge Graph**.

## Project Discussion

- The supervisors discussed the relationship between the Ontology and Knowledge Graph components.
- It was decided that the ontology and knowledge graph would be developed as a unified part of the project rather than as separate components.
- The main focus of the project is news forecasting supported by the ontology and knowledge graph.
- The news forecasting component would cover **four domains**, divided between the two pairs:
  - Domain Group 1: Yumna Aziz and Hamna Tariq (two domains)
  - Domain Group 2: Noor Fatima and Aqsa Bibi (the other two domains)
- The four specific domains were **not finalized or assigned** in this meeting.

## Key Decisions

1. The two existing groups would be merged into one four-member FYP team.
2. The whole team would work on the LLM-Integrated News Forecasting System Using Ontology & Knowledge Graph.
3. Ontology and Knowledge Graph would be developed as an integrated part of the project.
4. The news forecasting component would cover four domains.
5. Yumna Aziz and Hamna Tariq would work on two domains; Noor Fatima and Aqsa Bibi on the other two.
6. The specific domains would be decided at a later stage.

## Current Status

Initial planning and research stage.

## Next Steps

- Finalize the four news forecasting domains.
- Define the responsibilities of each team member.
- Further discuss the ontology and knowledge graph structure.
- Research relevant news forecasting approaches.
- Investigate suitable data sources.
- Define the initial system architecture and methodology.
